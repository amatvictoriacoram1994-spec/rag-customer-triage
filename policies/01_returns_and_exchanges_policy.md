NIVARA GOODS

RETURNS AND EXCHANGES POLICY

Document ID: POL-RET-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 15 July 2026
Document Type: Customer Support Policy


1. Purpose

This policy explains when customers may return or exchange products purchased directly from the Nivara Goods website.

2. Return Eligibility

Returns require a valid Nivara Goods order number, a timely request, eligible item condition, a delivered product, and no non-returnable exclusion. Third-party purchases must be handled by the original seller. Gift items are handled under the Gift Orders and Gift Returns Policy.

3. Return Window

Eligible products may be returned within 45 calendar days of the recorded delivery date. The request must be submitted before the end of the 45th calendar day. If tracking shows delivery on 1 August, the final day to submit a return request is 15 September. Requests after 45 days require human review and approval is not guaranteed.

4. Condition of Returned Items

Returned items must be unused or only reasonably inspected, free from stains, odours, scratches, dents, alterations, or extended use, and include original components, accessories, manuals, tags, and packaging where applicable.

5. Non-Returnable Items

Final-sale, personalised, gift card, free replacement part, misused, unverifiable, unsafe, or heavily used products are not eligible for a standard return. A promotional discount does not automatically make a product final sale.

6. Exchange Rules

Eligible products may be exchanged for a different colour, size, variant, or identical replacement within the return window, subject to availability. Only one exchange is permitted per order item.

7. Return Process

Customers must contact support, provide order and product details, provide photographs when relevant, wait for approval, pack securely, use approved shipping, and retain tracking.

8. Refund Processing Time

Refund processing begins after the returned product reaches the facility and passes inspection. Inspection normally takes five business days. Approved refunds are initiated within three business days, while banks may take five to ten business days to display funds.

9. Exceptions and Human Review

Escalate when the request is outside the return window, proof is missing, policies conflict, damaged or warranty policy may also apply, tracking is disputed, the item is altered or incomplete, fraud or chargeback risk exists, or the requested outcome is not covered.

10. Customer Support Contact

Customers should submit full name, order number, email, product name, reason, photographs where applicable, and preferred resolution. Submission does not confirm approval.
