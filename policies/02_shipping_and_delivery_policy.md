NIVARA GOODS

SHIPPING AND DELIVERY POLICY

Document ID: POL-SHP-002
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 15 July 2026
Document Type: Customer Support Policy


1. Purpose

This policy covers domestic order processing, dispatch, tracking, delivery, delays, address problems, failed attempts, missing packages, and human review.

2. Order Processing

Orders are normally processed within one to two business days after payment confirmation. Processing may take longer for custom products, payment checks, invalid addresses, stock issues, fraud review, or operational disruption.

3. Domestic Delivery Timelines

Standard delivery normally takes three to seven business days after dispatch. Express delivery normally takes one to three business days after dispatch. Estimates are not guaranteed.

4. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are displayed at checkout. Shipping charges are not normally refunded for change of mind, refusal without approval, incorrect address, or non-collection.

5. Tracking Information

Customers normally receive tracking after dispatch. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to activate. Contact support if tracking has not updated for more than five calendar days, the package is marked delivered but missing, or an exception appears.

6. Delivery Address Changes

Address changes may be requested before dispatch but are not guaranteed after processing begins. Changes involving another city, state, postal code, or country require review.

7. Delivery Attempts and Failed Delivery

Customers are responsible for accurate address, reachable phone, carrier communications, and package collection. Failed delivery may lead to redispatch charges and support approval.

8. Packages Marked Delivered but Not Received

Customers should check safe locations, reception, neighbours, and wait until end of day. A refund or replacement must not be automatically approved only because the package is reported missing.

9. Delayed Shipments

A delayed shipment is not automatically lost. Refunds or replacements should not be promised unless carrier evidence supports that outcome.

10. Lost Shipments

A shipment may be treated as lost when the carrier confirms loss or closes an investigation as lost. Nivara Goods may offer replacement, refund, or store credit when carrier or company error is confirmed.

11. Damaged Packages

Customers should photograph packaging, shipping label, internal packaging, product damage, and missing components. Product damage is handled under the Damaged or Defective Items Policy.

12. Exceptions and Human Review

Escalate when delivery is disputed, delay is not confirmed lost, address changed after dispatch, shipment returned to sender, compensation is requested, fraud or payment dispute exists, tracking is inconsistent, or policies conflict.

13. Customer Support Contact

Customers should provide full name, order number, email, address, tracking number, issue description, screenshots or photos, and preferred resolution.
