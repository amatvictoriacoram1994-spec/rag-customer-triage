NIVARA GOODS

PRODUCT WARRANTY POLICY

Document ID: POL-WAR-003
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 15 July 2026
Document Type: Customer Support Policy


1. Purpose

This policy explains the limited warranty for eligible Nivara Goods products, including defects, warranty periods, exclusions, evidence, resolutions, and human review.

2. Products Covered

The warranty applies to eligible physical products purchased directly from Nivara Goods or authorised sellers with valid proof of purchase and proper use.

3. Warranty Period

Backpacks, laptop bags, travel organisers, and similar fabric products are covered for 12 months from delivery. Insulated bottles and drinkware are covered for six months. Accessories and separately purchased parts are covered for three months.

4. Manufacturing Defects

Manufacturing defects include failures caused by materials, components, or production, such as stitching separation, zip or strap failure during normal use, insulation failure, leak caused by manufacturing fault, or missing component from packing error.

5. Warranty Exclusions

The warranty excludes normal wear, cosmetic damage, accidents, misuse, overloading, unauthorised repair, improper care, loss, airline damage, third-party damage, and preference-based dissatisfaction.

6. Reporting a Warranty Claim

Customers should submit full name, order number or proof, product name, delivery date, issue description, first appearance, usage context, photos or video, and previous resolution history.

7. Warranty Assessment

Approval depends on the cause and evidence. A claim is not approved only because damage occurred within the warranty period.

8. Available Resolutions

Approved claims may receive repair, component replacement, same or comparable product replacement, store credit, full refund, or partial refund. The customer cannot automatically choose any resolution.

9. Shipping and Inspection Costs

Nivara Goods may cover shipping when evidence suggests a manufacturing defect or return is requested for inspection and the claim is approved. Customers may pay if no covered defect is found.

10. Products Damaged Shortly After Delivery

A product reported damaged, defective, incomplete, or non-functional shortly after delivery may also be covered by the Returns and Exchanges Policy, Damaged or Defective Items Policy, or this Warranty Policy. This policy does not establish priority. The case must be escalated when more than one route may apply.

11. Warranty Claims After a Previous Exchange

Replacement items may still qualify for warranty support, but the original purchase date, warranty period, replacement delivery date, and prior claim history must be reviewed.

12. Proof of Purchase

Accepted proof includes a Nivara Goods order number, invoice from Nivara Goods, authorised seller invoice, or another independently verifiable record. A bank screenshot alone may not be enough.

13. Exceptions and Human Review

Escalate when cause is unclear, more than one policy may apply, proof is incomplete, warranty period has passed, product was altered, prior resolution exists, fraud or payment dispute is possible, or documents conflict.

14. Customer Support Contact

Submitting a warranty claim does not confirm coverage, a manufacturing defect, refund, replacement, or covered shipping.
