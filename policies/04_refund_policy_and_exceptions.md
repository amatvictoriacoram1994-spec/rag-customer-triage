NIVARA GOODS

REFUND POLICY AND EXCEPTIONS

Document ID: POL-REF-004
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 15 July 2026
Document Type: Customer Support Policy


1. Purpose

This policy explains refund timing, amounts, methods, exclusions, and human review. It does not independently establish return, warranty, cancellation, delivery, or damaged-item eligibility.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be issued when an eligible return is approved, pre-dispatch cancellation is completed, product is unavailable, shipment is confirmed lost due to carrier or company error, incorrect product refund is approved, damaged or defective product qualifies, duplicate charge is confirmed, or human review approves.

3. Refund Amount

Refunds may include product amount, taxes, required original shipping, and approved return shipping. Refunds may exclude change-of-mind shipping, express upgrades, gift wrapping, non-cash credits, deductions, and third-party fees.

4. Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may apply to multi-item returns, missing accessories, damage, price adjustments, bundled purchases, used benefits, or agreed partial resolutions. The system must not invent deduction amounts.

5. Original Payment Method

Refunds are normally issued to the original payment method. Multi-method payments may be refunded proportionally. Cash refunds are not normally issued for non-cash payments.

6. Gift Cards, Store Credit, and Promotional Credits

Gift-card amounts return to gift card or store credit. Promotional credits and vouchers normally have no cash value and may not be reissued.

7. Discounts and Bundled Purchases

Refundable amount is based on discounted amount actually paid. Bundle discounts and free products may be recalculated.

8. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges may be refunded for pre-dispatch cancellation, confirmed lost shipment, or full refund due to incorrect, damaged, or defective products. They are not normally refunded for change of mind or customer-caused delivery failure.

9. Refund Processing Timeline

Refund processing begins after approval. Payment method refunds normally initiate within three business days. Banks may take five to ten business days to display funds.

10. Failed, Rejected, or Unavailable Refund Methods

Alternative refund methods require review when the original account or payment instrument is unavailable or payment records cannot be verified.

11. Refunds for Cancelled Orders

Orders cancelled before dispatch may qualify for product, tax, and unused standard shipping refund. After dispatch, return policy may apply.

12. Duplicate and Incorrect Charges

Two pending entries do not always mean duplicate completed charges. The system must not promise a refund without payment evidence.

13. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

Active chargebacks pause direct refund handling. A separate refund must not be issued automatically because it may create duplicate reimbursement.

14. Refund Exceptions

Refunds may be denied, reduced, delayed, or reviewed when eligibility is unclear, window expired, product non-returnable, proof missing, prior resolution exists, fraud suspected, chargeback active, amount unclear, policies conflict, or method unsupported.

15. Exceptions and Human Review

Escalate when eligibility is not established, alternate payment method is requested, multiple payment methods were used, partial calculation is needed, promotions affect amount, chargeback is active, prior resolution may exist, or documents conflict.

16. Customer Support Contact

Customers should provide full name, order number, email, disputed product or charge, reason, payment records, and prior resolution details.
