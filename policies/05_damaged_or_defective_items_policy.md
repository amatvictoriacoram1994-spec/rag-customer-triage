NIVARA GOODS

DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS POLICY

Document ID: POL-DAM-005
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 15 July 2026
Document Type: Customer Support Policy


1. Purpose

This policy explains handling of products that arrive damaged, defective, incomplete, or incorrect.

2. Issues Covered

This policy may apply when a product arrives visibly damaged, non-functional, incorrect, missing components, broken upon delivery, materially different from the order, or damaged in shipping before receipt.

3. Reporting Period

Customers should report damaged, defective, incomplete, or incorrect products within seven calendar days of delivery. Reports after seven days are not automatically rejected and may require review under returns, warranty, or another policy.

4. Evidence Required

Customers should provide order information, description, date noticed, photos or videos, package images, shipping label, internal packaging, all items received, and details of missing components.

5. Packaging Requirements

Customers should keep original package, internal packaging, labels, and product until the claim is reviewed.

6. Initial Assessment

Nivara Goods reviews description, evidence, order details, tracking, batch information, packaging condition, prior claims, and whether the issue occurred before or after delivery.

7. Incorrect Products

Incorrect products include mismatched product name, colour, size, variant, quantity, wrong-customer product, or material difference from the ordered item.

8. Missing Items or Components

Missing-item claims are reviewed against order, product description, packing records, package weight, photos, and separate-shipment information.

9. Available Resolutions

Approved claims may lead to replacement, component replacement, repair, exchange, store credit, full refund, partial refund, or another reasonable resolution. The customer cannot automatically choose any resolution.

10. Return of the Affected Product

Nivara Goods may require return before replacement or refund. Nivara Goods normally covers return shipping when shipping, packing, manufacturing, or fulfilment error is confirmed.

11. Immediate Replacement Without Return

Replacement without return may be approved when return is impractical, low value, evidence is sufficient, shipment unsafe, return cost exceeds value, or manager approves. It is not automatic.

12. Refunds

A refund may be considered when replacement is unavailable, repair impractical, product is confirmed damaged or defective, incorrect product was received and replacement rejected, or other resolution cannot be completed.

13. Relationship With Returns and Warranty

A damaged or defective product may also fall under Returns, Warranty, and Shipping policies. There is no universal priority order. When multiple policies could lead to different outcomes, escalate.

14. Claims Reported After the Initial Period

Claims after seven days may still qualify based on issue, discovery timing, use, evidence, return window, warranty period, and possible misuse.

15. Suspected Misuse or Accidental Damage

Review is needed when evidence suggests impact, overloading, improper cleaning, unauthorised repair, modification, unsuitable exposure, third-party damage, or continued use.

16. Duplicate or Repeated Claims

Additional review is required for prior refunds, replacements, repeated similar claims, chargebacks, inconsistent records, or suspected fraud.

17. Exceptions and Human Review

Escalate when cause or timing is unclear, evidence incomplete, multiple policies apply, seven-day period passed, misuse is disputed, compensation beyond policy is requested, prior resolution exists, carrier investigation is required, chargeback active, policies conflict, or outcome not covered.

18. Customer Support Contact

Submitting a report does not confirm approval, defect status, refund, replacement, covered shipping, or right to keep the affected product.
