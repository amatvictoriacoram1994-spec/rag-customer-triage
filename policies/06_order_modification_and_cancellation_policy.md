NIVARA GOODS

ORDER MODIFICATION AND CANCELLATION POLICY

Document ID: POL-CAN-006
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 15 July 2026
Document Type: Customer Support Policy


1. Purpose

This policy explains order changes and cancellations for Nivara Goods website orders.

2. Order Processing Stages

Stages include payment pending, payment confirmed, processing, packed, dispatched, delivered, cancelled, and returned to sender. Options depend on current stage.

3. Cancellation Before Dispatch

Customers may request cancellation before dispatch. Cancellation is not guaranteed. Successful pre-dispatch cancellation may refund product amount, taxes, and unused standard shipping.

4. Cancellation After Dispatch

Orders cannot normally be cancelled after dispatch. The customer may need to accept delivery and request a return. The system must not promise cancellation or immediate refund after dispatch.

5. Product Changes

Before dispatch, customers may request colour, size, variant, item removal, item addition, or product replacement. Approval depends on stock, stage, price, tax, shipping, promotion, and warehouse status.

6. Quantity Changes

Quantity changes may affect payment, shipping, taxes, promotions, free shipping, bundle discounts, gifts, and refund amounts. The system must not promise final adjustment when unclear.

7. Delivery Address Changes

Address changes may be requested before dispatch but are not guaranteed after processing starts. Major address changes may affect charges, taxes, duties, and carrier availability.

8. Contact Information Changes

Name, phone, email, or delivery instructions may be corrected. Identity or payment owner changes require verification.

9. Personalised and Custom Products

Personalised products may enter production quickly. Cancellation or modification may not be possible after production starts.

10. Promotional Orders

Changes or cancellations may affect discounts, bundles, free gifts, store credit, referrals, free shipping, and minimum spend. Used discounts are not automatically restored.

11. Payment Adjustments

Increases may require additional payment. Decreases may follow Refund Policy. A modification is complete only after payment, inventory, system update, and confirmation.

12. Duplicate Orders

Similar orders are not automatically duplicates. Review order numbers, products, payments, timestamps, address, and stage.

13. Failed Cancellation or Modification Requests

Requests may be declined after dispatch, after custom production starts, when items unavailable, address unsupported, financial impact unclear, fraud review active, or verification insufficient.

14. Relationship With Other Policies

Refunds, dispatched orders, delivery issues, international changes, and payment disputes may require other policies.

15. Exceptions and Human Review

Escalate when order stage unclear, cancellation after dispatch, taxes or promotions affected, custom production started, timing disputed, unauthorised order claimed, identity changes involved, chargeback active, policies conflict, or outcome not covered.

16. Customer Support Contact

Submitting a request does not guarantee modification, cancellation, or refund.
