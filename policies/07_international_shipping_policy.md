NIVARA GOODS

INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING POLICY

Document ID: POL-INT-007
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 15 July 2026
Document Type: Customer Support Policy


1. Purpose

This policy covers orders shipped outside India, including destinations, customs, duties, address requirements, delays, refused delivery, returns, and human review.

2. Available Destinations

International shipping is available only to eligible countries and regions shown at checkout. Availability may depend on product type, address, carrier coverage, import restrictions, size, payment verification, trade restrictions, and disruption.

3. Order Processing

International orders normally process within two to four business days after payment confirmation. Verification, customs documentation, address issues, restricted items, fraud review, and carrier issues may delay processing.

4. International Delivery Estimates

International delivery normally takes seven to twenty business days after dispatch. Customs time is outside Nivara Goods’ direct control. Estimates are not guaranteed.

5. Shipping Charges

International shipping charges are shown at checkout and do not automatically include duties, taxes, brokerage, or local handling unless clearly stated.

6. Customs Duties and Import Taxes

The destination country may charge duties, taxes, brokerage, inspection, local handling, and other fees. Unless checkout says included, the customer is responsible. The system must not calculate specific customs amounts without reliable destination information.

7. Customs Documentation

Customers must not ask Nivara Goods to understate value, mark commercial purchases as gifts, use false descriptions, remove required information, avoid duties, or provide inaccurate information.

8. Customer Information Requirements

International customers must provide full recipient name, complete address, postal code, country, phone, email, and tax or ID number where required.

9. Address Changes

Changing to another country may require cancellation and reorder because shipping, taxes, eligibility, carriers, and customs documents may change.

10. Restricted and Prohibited Products

Some products may not be eligible internationally due to destination, carrier, safety, material, size, battery, liquid, labelling, or licensing restrictions.

11. Customs Delays

Customs delays do not automatically mean a shipment is lost. Nivara Goods must not promise an unconfirmed release date.

12. Unpaid Duties or Taxes

If required charges are unpaid, shipment may be held, returned, abandoned, destroyed, or charged fees. A refund is not automatically due.

13. Refused Deliveries

Customers should not refuse international delivery without contacting Nivara Goods. Refusal does not automatically qualify for full refund.

14. International Tracking

Tracking may activate after 48 hours, show limited customs updates, transfer to local carrier, show local language messages, or pause between countries.

15. Lost International Shipments

International shipments are lost only when carrier or reliable evidence confirms loss. Delays are not automatically lost.

16. Damaged International Shipments

Customers should report damage and provide package, shipping label, customs label, internal packaging, product, and component photos.

17. International Returns

International returns require prior approval and may involve return shipping, customs, export, import, handling, tracking, and insurance costs.

18. Refunds for International Orders

Eligibility must first be established by applicable policy. Refunds may exclude international shipping, express upgrades, duties, brokerage, conversion fees, return shipping, storage, handling, and refusal or incorrect-address charges.

19. Exceptions and Human Review

Escalate when import requirements unclear, duties disputed, customs delay, unpaid charges, return or destruction, country change, deductions unclear, delivery disputed, carrier investigation open, policies conflict, or outcome not covered.

20. Customer Support Contact

Customers should provide order, country, address, tracking, issue, customs or carrier communication, photos, and preferred resolution.
