NIVARA GOODS

GIFT ORDERS AND GIFT RETURNS POLICY

Document ID: POL-GFT-008
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 15 July 2026
Document Type: Customer Support Policy


1. Purpose

This policy explains gift orders, gift receipts, gift returns, exchanges, refunds, privacy, promotional gifts, and human review.

2. Gift Order Eligibility

A product may be treated as a gift when gift option is selected, gift receipt requested, valid order number confirms gift intent, or Nivara Goods verifies it. A different delivery address does not automatically make a gift.

3. Gift Messages and Packaging

Gift messages and packaging may be available. Gift packaging charges are not normally refundable after completion.

4. Prices and Gift Receipts

Gift receipts may exclude price while showing product, order reference, delivery date, instructions, and purchase-identifying information.

5. Gift Return Window

Eligible gifts may be returned within 45 calendar days of delivery and must meet Returns and Exchanges Policy condition and eligibility requirements. Late requests require human review.

6. Proof of Gift Purchase

Gift returns may be verified using gift receipt, order number, purchaser-provided verifiable record, or another independently matchable record. A payment screenshot alone may not identify product, date, or seller.

7. Condition of Gift Returns

Gift items must be reasonably inspected only, include components and accessories, be free from damage or extended use, include tags and packaging where available, not be personalised or final sale, and be packed securely.

8. Gift Exchanges

Eligible recipients may request colour, size, variant, identical replacement, or another product of equal or lower value when approved. Lower price difference is normally store credit.

9. Refunds for Gift Returns

Gift refunds are normally handled by refund to purchaser’s original method, store credit to recipient, gift-card balance to recipient, or another approved resolution. Gift recipient is not automatically entitled to cash or refund to their own account.

10. Purchaser Privacy

Nivara Goods must not disclose purchaser private information to recipient without valid reason or permission.

11. Damaged or Defective Gifts

Damaged or defective gifts may require review under damaged items, warranty, shipping, and returns policies. When multiple policies apply and resolutions differ, escalate.

12. Promotional Gifts and Free Products

Free promotional gifts may need to be returned if qualifying purchase is returned. The value of unreturned promotional gift may affect refund when terms allow.

13. Gift Cards

Gift cards are governed by terms. They are not normally redeemable for cash, lost cards may not be replaceable, and suspected unauthorised use requires review.

14. International Gift Orders

International gift orders remain subject to international shipping, customs duties, import taxes, restrictions, carrier requirements, return costs, and address or ID requirements.

15. Exceptions and Human Review

Escalate when purchase cannot be matched, return window expired, purchaser and recipient request different outcomes, refund would disclose privacy, recipient requests cash to different account, multiple payments were used, promotion affects refund, multiple policies apply, gift card is lost or stolen, policies conflict, or outcome not covered.

16. Customer Support Contact

Gift purchasers and recipients should provide name, receipt or order number, product, delivery date, reason, evidence, preferred resolution, and whether they are purchaser or recipient.
