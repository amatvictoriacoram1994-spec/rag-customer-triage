NIVARA GOODS

PROMOTIONS AND DISCOUNT TERMS

Document ID: POL-PRO-009
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 15 July 2026
Document Type: Customer Support Policy


1. Purpose

This policy explains discount codes, promotional pricing, free gifts, bundle offers, referral benefits, store credit, and other promotions.

2. General Eligibility

Promotions apply only when all stated conditions are met, such as minimum value, eligible products, account, location, period, payment method, customer status, code, and usage limits.

3. Promotion Period

Promotions apply only during stated dates and times unless otherwise specified. They may end early if inventory or redemption limits are reached.

4. Discount Codes

Discount codes must be entered before payment. Codes may be case-sensitive and restricted by customer, product, location, order value, or account.

5. Combining Promotions

Only one discount code may be used unless terms allow combination. The system must not promise combination unless terms clearly allow it.

6. Minimum Order Value

Minimum order value is normally calculated using eligible products after exclusions and discounts. Shipping, taxes, gift cards, store credit, final-sale items, and free items may not count.

7. Product and Category Restrictions

Promotions may exclude new launches, limited editions, personalised products, gift cards, replacement parts, final sale, selected collections, discounted products, and third-party products.

8. Free Gifts

Free gifts are subject to availability, not redeemable for cash, not automatically exchangeable, not automatically covered by purchased-item refund value, and linked to qualifying purchase.

9. Bundle Offers

Returning part of a bundle may reprice remaining products, remove discount, require returning free product, and alter refund. Refund cannot exceed amount paid.

10. Referral Promotions

Referral benefits may be denied for ineligible purchase, cancellation, fraud, same-person accounts, unauthorised sharing, limits, or waiting periods.

11. First-Order Promotions

First-order eligibility may be assessed using account, email, phone, address, payment method, device, and order history. New account does not guarantee eligibility.

12. Promotional Pricing Errors

Nivara Goods may correct or cancel incorrect, technical-error, overused, excluded-product, obviously unintended, fraudulent, or misused promotions. The system must not promise erroneous price will be honoured.

13. Cancelled and Modified Orders

Order changes may affect discounts, free shipping, free gifts, bundles, referral benefits, and payment amount. Discount codes are not automatically restored.

14. Returns and Refunds

Discounted product refunds are based on amount paid. Discounts are not refunded as cash. Codes may not be reissued. Bundle and free-product terms may be recalculated.

15. Expired, Unused, or Failed Discounts

Nivara Goods is not normally required to replace expired, forgotten, mistyped, ineligible, limit-reached, account-linked, unauthorised, or cancelled-order discounts.

16. Technical Problems

Customers should provide code, screenshot, cart products, order value, date and time, account email, and device or browser information. A screenshot alone does not prove eligibility.

17. Misuse and Fraud

Promotional benefits may be cancelled for multiple accounts, referral manipulation, unauthorised publication, bulk redemption, false information, refund abuse, duplicate benefits, or violations.

18. Exceptions and Human Review

Escalate when specific terms unavailable, promotions conflict, technical failure claimed, return affects bundles or gifts, expired code restoration requested, eligibility disputed, pricing error possible, referral unusual, fraud suspected, recalculation needed, policies conflict, or outcome not covered.

19. Customer Support Contact

Customers should provide name, email, order number, code, promotion name, products, screenshot, date/time, and requested resolution.
