NIVARA GOODS

CHARGEBACK AND PAYMENT DISPUTE POLICY

Document ID: POL-CHG-010
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 15 July 2026
Document Type: Customer Support Policy


1. Purpose

This policy explains chargebacks, payment disputes, unauthorised transaction claims, duplicate charges, refund disputes, and payment-provider investigations.

2. Issues Covered

This policy applies to claims of unauthorised transaction, goods not received, product not as described, refund not received, duplicate charge, wrong amount, cancelled order still charged, returned product not refunded, failed order payment, or other payment-provider dispute.

3. Customer Contact Before a Chargeback

Customers are encouraged to contact Nivara Goods before opening a chargeback. Support may review order, payment, refund, delivery, cancellation, return, warranty, damaged item, and escalation status.

4. Active Chargebacks

When a chargeback or payment dispute is active, a separate refund, replacement, or store credit must not be issued automatically. The case must be escalated and records preserved.

5. Unauthorised Transaction Claims

The system must not accuse the customer of fraud and must not confirm an unauthorised payment without reliable evidence. Escalate for secure review.

6. Duplicate Charge Claims

Two similar payment entries may be temporary authorisation, failed attempt, reversed transaction, pending transaction, or separate valid order. Duplicate refund must not be promised without payment confirmation.

7. Refund Not Received Claims

Review refund approval, initiation, amount, original method, provider reference, failure status, chargeback status, and bank processing period before promising another refund.

8. Goods Not Received Disputes

Review tracking, delivery proof, address, signature, photo, communication, exceptions, return-to-sender, and carrier investigation. Tracking delivered does not automatically prove personal receipt.

9. Product Not as Described Disputes

Review listing, confirmation, variant, evidence, customer description, return history, and materiality. Do not decide when evidence is incomplete or disputed.

10. Cancelled Order Disputes

Review cancellation request time, order stage, confirmation, dispatch time, payment status, refund status, and communications. Request is not confirmed cancellation.

11. Returned Product Disputes

Review return approval, tracking, warehouse receipt, inspection, refund decision, deductions, initiation, payment-provider status, and return eligibility.

12. Evidence and Records

Evidence may include order confirmation, payment confirmation, invoice, tracking, delivery proof, return tracking, inspection notes, refund records, messages, photographs, policy terms, and provider correspondence.

13. Customer Communication During a Dispute

Communication should confirm review, avoid unsupported admission, avoid fraud accusations, explain direct refund limits, request missing information, and avoid promising provider-controlled outcomes.

14. Refunds During a Payment Dispute

Any direct refund during active chargeback requires human approval to avoid duplicate reimbursement.

15. Chargeback Outcome

The payment provider may decide for either side, request evidence, close dispute, reverse credit, return funds, or apply fees. Nivara Goods does not control provider decision or timeline.

16. Closed or Withdrawn Disputes

Do not assume dispute closure only because customer says they contacted bank. Provider confirmation may be required.

17. Suspected Fraud or Abuse

Additional review is required for multiple disputes, non-delivery conflict, prior compensation, shared payment details, altered evidence, repeated patterns, provider fraud report, or account takeover concerns.

18. Relationship With Other Policies

Underlying issues may require shipping, returns, refund, damaged items, warranty, cancellation, international, or gift policies. This policy governs payment dispute process, not underlying claim validity.

19. Exceptions and Human Review

Escalate when chargeback active, payment status unclear, refund may already exist, unauthorised transaction reported, duplicate reimbursement possible, delivery evidence disputed, return not matched, closure unverified, fraud suspected, provider requests evidence, policies conflict, or outcome not covered.

20. Customer Support Contact

Customers should provide name, order number, email, transaction date, amount, payment method, dispute description, reference, screenshots, and previous resolution details. Do not share full card numbers, passwords, or OTPs.
