NIVARA GOODS

RETURNS POLICY

Document ID: POL-RET-001
Version: 0.9
Effective Date: 1 June 2026
Document Type: Customer Support Policy


1. Purpose

This policy explains the conditions under which customers may return products purchased directly from the Nivara Goods website.

2. Return Eligibility

Products may be returned when purchased directly from Nivara Goods, valid order number is provided, request is within window, product is not non-returnable, and condition standards are met.

3. Return Window

Eligible products may be returned within 30 calendar days of the recorded delivery date. The customer must submit the request before the end of the 30th calendar day. Requests after 30 days are not eligible unless Nivara Goods approves an exception. If delivered on 1 August, the final day is 31 August.

4. Product Condition

Unless damaged or defective on arrival, returned item must be unused, include original packaging, accessories, components, and tags, and be free from stains, damage, alterations, or signs of use.

5. Non-Returnable Products

Final-sale products, personalised products, gift cards, store credit, products damaged through misuse, products without proof of purchase, and products returned after 30 days cannot be returned.

6. Exchanges

Eligible products may be exchanged within 30 calendar days of delivery, subject to availability. If unavailable, refund or store credit may be offered.

7. Return Shipping

Customers pay return shipping for change of mind, wrong size or colour, or accidental order. Nivara Goods may cover return shipping for wrong, damaged, defective, or incomplete orders.

8. Refund Processing

Refunds are processed after product reaches facility and passes inspection. Approved refunds are normally initiated within five business days.

9. Exceptions

Damaged products, warranty issues, payment disputes, missing delivery records, or unusual circumstances may require additional review.

10. Customer Support Contact

Customers should provide full name, order number, product name, reason, photographs, and preferred resolution. Submission does not guarantee approval.
