NIVARA GOODS

REPLACEMENT AND SPARE PARTS POLICY

Document ID: POL-RPL-012
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 15 July 2026
Document Type: Customer Support Policy


1. Purpose

This policy explains replacement products, replacement components, spare parts, eligibility, availability, charges, shipping, warranty treatment, and human review.

2. Replacement Products

Replacement may be considered when original product arrived damaged or defective, wrong product was delivered, shipment was confirmed lost, approved warranty cannot be repaired, approved exchange requires identical replacement, or human review approves.

3. Replacement Components

Components may include straps, buckles, lids, caps, handles, zips or zip pulls, fasteners, inserts, packaging components, or other detachable parts. Availability depends on design, compatibility, stock, and reason.

4. Spare Parts for Purchase

Selected spare parts may be available for purchase depending on model, age, variant, supplier inventory, safety, destination, and installation needs.

5. Required Information

Customers should provide name, order number or proof, product name, model, delivery date, part description, photos, serial or batch label, reason, and address.

6. Compatibility

The system must not confirm compatibility when model unclear, evidence missing, multiple versions exist, product modified, part designed for another product, or installation safety risk exists.

7. Free Replacements

Free replacement may apply for incomplete original order, wrong component, covered manufacturing defect, approved warranty resolution, fulfilment error, or approved exception. It is not automatic due to return or warranty period alone.

8. Paid Replacements

Customer may pay when part lost after delivery, damage from misuse or accident, warranty expired, optional accessory requested, additional component requested, shipping applies, or no covered fault found.

9. Replacement Availability

Identical replacement may be unavailable due to discontinuation, variant out of production, temporary stock issues, supplier ending production, design changes, or shipping restrictions. Alternatives must not be promised before confirmation.

10. Shipping Charges

Nivara Goods may cover shipping for incomplete order, wrong component, covered manufacturing defect, or approved free replacement shipping. Customer may pay for loss, misuse, optional parts, expired warranty, no covered fault, or international shipping.

11. Return of the Original Product or Component

Nivara Goods may require original product or component return before replacement. Replacement may be delayed until return or inspection is completed.

12. Replacement Processing Time

Processing begins after eligibility, evidence, required return or inspection, inventory, and any payment are completed. Approved replacements normally prepare within three business days.

13. Warranty for Replacement Products and Components

A replacement product does not automatically receive a new full warranty. It remains covered for the longer of original remaining warranty or ninety calendar days from replacement delivery.

14. Repeated Replacement Requests

Review is required when product already replaced, multiple components requested, same issue repeated, evidence conflicts, refund or credit already issued, chargeback linked, or fraud suspected.

15. Relationship With Other Policies

Replacement eligibility may depend on returns, warranty, damaged items, shipping, refund, and international policies. When multiple policies apply and could produce different outcomes, escalate.

16. Exceptions and Human Review

Escalate when compatibility unclear, identical replacement unavailable, product modified, second replacement requested, responsibility disputed, charges unclear, international duties apply, prior resolution exists, policies conflict, or outcome not covered.

17. Customer Support Contact

Customers should provide name, order number, product name and model, required component, photos, delivery address, reason, and preferred resolution.
